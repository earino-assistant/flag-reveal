Flag Party

Guess the country from a half-revealed flag.

  1. 1

    Everyone grabs a phone — one player taps Start a party, the rest join with the 6-letter code.

  2. 2

    The flag reveals in steps — a grid of tiles clears and the blur sharpens. The earlier you recognise it, the more it's worth.

  3. 3

    Ring in the moment you recognise it — the first correct ring wins the round.

  4. 4

    Guess wrong and you're out for the round — but nobody sees what you guessed until the reveal. 😅

  5. 5

    Put it on the TV, or play today's Daily solo — highest total wins the crown.
