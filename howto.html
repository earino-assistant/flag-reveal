Flag Party

Guess the country from a half-revealed flag.

  1. Everyone grabs a phone. One player taps Start a party to make a room; everyone else joins with the 6-letter code. Optionally put a TV on the big screen at screen.html (or tap Add a TV).
  2. The flag reveals in steps. A grid of tiles clears and a blur sharpens across 8 steps. The earlier you recognise the country, the more it's worth (1000 at step 1 down to 125 at step 8).
  3. Ring in. Type the country (or, in easy mode, tap one of four choices once they unlock). The first correct ring wins the round. Guess wrong and you're locked out for the round — but nobody sees what you rang until the reveal. 😅
  4. Nobody gets it? The round busts after the flag is fully shown — and the answer is revealed anyway.
  5. Highest total after all the flags wins the crown. The winner hosts the next game.
